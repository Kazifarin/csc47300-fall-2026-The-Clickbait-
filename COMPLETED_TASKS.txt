11. IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a finished classroom frontend demo, not an official campus directory.
- All schedules, building associations, and schematic positions remain unverified
  examples. Replace them with verified information before real student use.
- The pantry intentionally has unknown hours. Holidays, breaks, appointment slots,
  emergency response, and exceptional closures are not modeled.
- Map positions are percentages, not geographic coordinates; there is no turn-by-turn
  routing. External directions use a labeled Google Maps place search.
- Reviews/bookmarks are local to the browser and origin; they are not shared online.
- Tabs do not synchronize changes live; refresh to pick up another tab's changes.
- A QA entry named 'Demo QA' and a bookmark were used in the verification browser;
  they are browser-local and are not part of the source dataset.
- Offline-ready means no network data/tile/image/font calls for core functionality
  once loaded. There is no service worker/PWA installation or offline-cache guarantee.
- No real-device or Safari testing, full WCAG audit, Lighthouse benchmark, or actual
  external destination verification was performed.
- No Git commit, push, PR, or public deployment was requested or performed.
- No token exhaustion was necessary: the requested implementation and report were
  completed and verified before ending the session.
